![文章配图](/media/f1039e66-304f-4a4e-a902-e0752d8e6fa6.png)

y = 4 \* \(\(speed - k\) - \(speed-k\)\*abs\(speed-k\)\)

k = floor\(speed\*0\.5\+0\.5\) \* 2

```text
float2 speed =  (IN.texcoord * _WaveTiling) + float2(_WaveSpeedX,_WaveSpeedY) * _Time.y;
float2 k = floor(speed*0.5+0.5) * 2;
float2 a = 4 * ((speed - k) - (speed-k)*abs(speed-k));
IN.texcoord.x += clamp(a * _WaveIntensity,-1,1);
IN.texcoord.y += clamp(a * _WaveIntensity,-1,1) * _WaveSpeedY;
half4 color = (tex2D(_MainTex, IN.texcoord) + _TextureSampleAdd) * IN.color;
```

### 拟合思路

首先这个函数的结果在\[-1,1\]区间是对的

**y = x - x \* abs\(x\)**
